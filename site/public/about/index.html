OYN

UI you can feel

OYN brings WebGPU materials to native HTML. A button keeps its text, keyboard focus, and click behavior while a shader draws its surface. Use OYN with plain DOM or React.

Materials

Ceramic
Raise a button to catch the light, or recess a track to hold a shadow.
Particle
Show loading content as fine grains that scatter when the content is ready.

OYN is a work in progress. Try the Ceramic study, or explore the source on GitHub.
